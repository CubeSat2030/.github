## What is CubeSat2030?

**CubeSat2030 is an independent spacecraft development project.**
The long-term goal is to eventually build and fly a small spacecraft in **Low Earth Orbit**.

---

## The Idea

Spacecraft are incredibly complex.

They bring together many different areas of engineering into something that has to work far away from the people who built it.

CubeSat2030 is an opportunity to explore that challenge one step at a time.

Rather than assuming everything is already known, the project follows a simple cycle:

**Learn → Experiment → Build → Test → Improve**

---

## Beyond the Workbench

The path toward space doesn't start with a rocket.

It starts with Research & Development.

CubeSat2030 also explores **high-altitude balloon missions** as a way to gain experience with real hardware, sensors, communications, software, and operating payloads outside the normal laboratory environment.

These experiments provide an opportunity to learn what happens when a year of R&D work leaves the workbench and enters the stratosphere.
